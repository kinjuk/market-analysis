03 Flags to Revisit
P/NAV Multiples — Underestimates MAIN's Premium MAIN trades at 1.67x NAV — a premium the peer median of 1.024x simply doesn't capture. No pure external BDC comp justifies MAIN's multiple. HTGC (1.38x) is the closest internally managed peer but still falls short. The P/NAV model ($34.25) should be read as a floor based on sector norms, not a fair value target. MAIN has traded above 1.5x NAV for years and the market has consistently rewarded its internal management, private equity income, and dividend consistency.
DDM Sensitivity — Supplemental Haircut Matters The DDM output of $75.50 uses a 75% haircut on supplemental dividends ($0.45/yr vs. $0.60 historical). At 100% inclusion, DDM rises to $84.10. At 50% haircut, it falls to $66.90. The supplemental assumption moves fair value by ~$9 per 25% change in the haircut. This is the largest single assumption risk in the model.
Three Models Converge at Current Price NII DCF ($54.75), Graham ($55.78), and Yield Model ($55.85) all cluster within $1.10 of each other and within $1.00 of today's price. This is a rare and meaningful signal — three independent methodologies saying the same thing. MAIN is not a bargain at $55.76, but it is not overpriced either. It is priced to perfection on income metrics.
Rate Cut Risk — Private Equity Income Cushion Unlike pure-play debt BDCs, MAIN generates meaningful income from its private equity co-investment portfolio (~25% of portfolio). This equity income is not interest-rate sensitive and partially offsets NII compression from rate cuts. Q1 2026 NII of $0.91–$0.95 is already reflecting rate pressure, but DNII of $0.98–$1.02 (which includes equity gains) remains above the base dividend of $0.795/quarter.
Internal Management — The Structural Moat MAIN has never paid an external management fee since its 2007 IPO. This structural advantage compounds over time — management incentives are aligned with shareholders rather than AUM growth. It is the primary reason MAIN deserves a premium to peers and why the P/NAV multiples model systematically understates its value.
