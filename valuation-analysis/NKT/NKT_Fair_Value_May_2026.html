03 Flags to Revisit
Energy Transition Premium — The Market Is Paying for the Future NKT trades at 36.2x forward earnings vs. peers at 20-21x. That 15x premium reflects the market's conviction that NKT is one of the primary beneficiaries of European grid infrastructure buildout — offshore wind interconnectors, HVDC corridors, and the energy transition. The premium is not irrational, but at DKK 1,080 vs a 4-model average of DKK 824, you are paying for execution that hasn't happened yet.
Graham Is the Only Model Supporting Current Price DCF (DKK 735), Multiples (DKK 627) and Lynch (DKK 600) all show overvaluation. Graham alone outputs DKK 1,334 — above current price — because it rewards NKT's 20% EPS growth aggressively. Graham's formula is optimistic by design for high-growth industrials. Use it as the bull ceiling, not a central estimate.
FCF Is Weak Relative to EBITDA NKT's operational EBITDA guidance for 2025 is €330-380M — but FCF is estimated at only ~€130M. The gap reflects massive capex investment in factory expansions (Cologne, Karlskrona, new submarine cable plant). This is a choice, not a problem — NKT is investing in capacity to capture the order backlog. But it means the DCF model captures the cost of that investment directly, producing the lowest fair value output of all four models.
Furukawa P/E Excluded from Median Furukawa Electric's 52.4x trailing P/E reflects a recent sharp re-rating driven by Japanese energy policy, not directly comparable to NKT's European cable business. Including it would push the peer median to 28.5x and the multiples fair value to DKK 855 — just below current price. The choice to use the European peer median (20.9x) is conservative but more defensible as a like-for-like comparison.
FX Note — EUR Reporting, DKK Listing NKT reports all financials in EUR. The DKK is pegged to the EUR at approximately 7.46, maintained by Danmarks Nationalbank. FX risk between DKK and EUR is structurally minimal due to the peg — but peer conversions from JPY carry normal currency exposure. All figures in this report use EUR/DKK 7.46 and JPY/DKK 0.0463.
