03 Flags to Revisit
AFFO DCF · Terminal Value Dominates The AFFO DCF outputs $88.75 — the highest of all five models — because at a 7.5% discount rate and 3% growth, the terminal value ($53.51) accounts for 60% of the fair value. A 50bps increase in the discount rate to 8.0% drops the fair value to ~$75. Treat the DCF as an optimistic anchor, not a central estimate. The yield model and P/AFFO multiples are more grounded in current market pricing.
Interest Rate Sensitivity REITs are inversely correlated with interest rates. O's A- credit rating and $65B market cap provide resilience, but the stock's 52-week range of $54.38–$67.93 reflects exactly this dynamic. Every 50bps move in the 10-year Treasury moves O's valuation by approximately $3–5. The current yield of 5.07% vs. the 10-year Treasury at ~4.3% gives a 77bps spread — historically tight, suggesting limited re-rating upside from yield compression.
P/AFFO Multiples · O Trades at a Premium to Peers O currently trades at 14.47x fwd AFFO vs. the peer median of 12.95x. The premium reflects scale (15,500+ properties), A- credit rating, 31-year Aristocrat streak, and monthly dividend cadence. The model applies peer median and outputs $57.33 — implying O deserves no premium over peers. Whether that premium is justified is the central valuation debate for this stock.
Walgreens & Tenant Concentration Risk Walgreens (closing stores aggressively) and Dollar General represent meaningful tenant exposure. O's 1,300+ tenant diversification limits single-name risk, but pharmacy chain closures require active portfolio management. Q1 2026 occupancy of 98.7% remains strong, providing a buffer.
Average · Weighting For a net lease REIT, the three most appropriate models are DDM, P/AFFO Multiples, and the Yield Model — all income-anchored. Their simple average is $66.42, which aligns closely with the analyst consensus of $66.75 and sits comfortably above the current price. The AFFO DCF and Graham pull the 5-model average up and down respectively from this anchor.
