03 Flags to Revisit
DDM · Model Drag DDM produces $125.55 — materially below the other models — because QCOM's payout ratio is low relative to earnings and dividend growth has slowed to ~7%. DDM captures income value, not the full earnings power. It is included but acts as a floor, not a central estimate.
Apple Modem Risk Apple is developing its own modem and is expected to transition away from QCOM's chips, putting an estimated $7B in annual revenue at risk over 2–3 years. This is already reflected in the depressed current price but not modelled explicitly in any of the five frameworks here.
DCF · FCF 2021 Spike FCF jumped +85.7% in 2021 due to a licensing settlement windfall — not recurring. The 10-year average FCF growth is distorted upward by this event. The 12% projection used here is conservative relative to that average, but worth flagging.
Multiples · MediaTek FX MediaTek trades in TWD. The forward P/E estimate of 40x is derived from TTM figures converted at current USD/TWD rates. FX movements will affect comparability. Treat this input as approximate.
Average · Weighting DDM and Lynch both anchor the average low. The DCF and Multiples outputs ($394 and $340) are the more structurally sound estimates for a fabless semiconductor with QCOM's FCF profile. A weighted approach would raise the fair value meaningfully.
