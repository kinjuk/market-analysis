03 Flags to Revisit
DCF · Capex Cycle Distortion TXN's 2023–2024 FCF collapsed 77% due to a deliberate multi-year manufacturing capex buildout. The DCF base uses management's guided 2026 FCF recovery (~$7.3B), not the depressed trailing figures. This is the single biggest model risk — if FCF recovery is slower, fair value is lower.
Lynch · Model Applicability Peter Lynch's FV = EPS × growth rate penalises mature compounders with moderate growth. At 16.3% growth, TXN scores $114 — a floor, not a target. Treat this output as a stress test, not a central case.
Multiples · STM & ON Distortion STM and ON are in earnings troughs with TTM P/Es above 200x. Forward P/E was used instead — cleaner, but still reflects cyclical recovery assumptions that may not materialise on schedule.
Average · Weighting All five models receive equal weight regardless of applicability. The DDM is the most structurally sound model for TXN given its 22-year dividend growth streak. To be revisited with a weighted scoring approach.
