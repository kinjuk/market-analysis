03 Flags to Revisit
Income Models vs Market Models — The Central Tension DDM ($53.49) and AFFO DCF ($55.39) both produce outputs nearly double the current price. The three market-anchored models — P/AFFO ($35.51), Graham ($35.60), Yield ($34.62) — cluster tightly at a $35.24 average, also well above $28.84 but far more conservative. VICI is below every single model output. The question is not whether it is undervalued — it clearly is — but by how much. The income models say 85%. The market models say 22%. The truth is probably somewhere between the two.
Gaming Tenant Concentration VICI's top four tenants — Caesars (38% of rent), MGM Resorts (33%), Venetian (10%), and Hard Rock (4%) — represent approximately 85% of total revenues. This is extreme concentration by any REIT standard. The flip side: these are long-term triple-net leases with 39.7-year weighted average lease terms and 100% rent collection since IPO including through COVID. The risk is real but partially mitigated by lease structure and tenant scale.
Interest Rate Sensitivity VICI carries $17.1B in total debt at a net leverage of 5.0x — higher than O (4.8x) but in line with GLPI. The 52-week range of $26.55–$34.01 reflects the rate sensitivity clearly. At the current yield of 6.24% vs. the 10-year Treasury at ~4.3%, VICI offers a 194bps spread — historically wide, suggesting the market is pricing in more risk than the fundamentals justify.
DDM Sensitivity — r Minus g Spread The DDM output of $53.49 uses r=7.5% and g=4.0%, giving a 3.5% spread. If the required return rises to 8.0% (justified if rates stay higher for longer), fair value drops to $40.04. If g rises to 5% (justified given VICI's 8.6% historical AFFO growth), fair value rises to $63.18. The DDM range is wide — treat it as directional, not precise.
VICI Trades at a Discount to Its Own Peers VICI's current P/AFFO of 11.75x is below every peer in the multiples table — GLPI (11.75x, effectively equal), EPRT (16.3x) and O (14.47x). Within its own net-lease REIT peer group, VICI is the cheapest on a forward earnings basis. The gaming concentration discount is real, but at 11.75x vs. a sector median of 14.47x, it appears excessive relative to VICI's credit quality and lease duration.
